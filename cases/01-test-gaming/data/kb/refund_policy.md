# Refund Policy

Customers on monthly plans may request a full refund within 14 days of their most recent charge. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Approved refunds typically appear on the customer statement within 5 to 7 business days. Bank processing times are outside our control.

Annual plans cancelled after the first 14 days are refunded on a prorated basis. A processing fee of 5 percent is deducted from prorated annual refunds.
